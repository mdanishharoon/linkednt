# PRODUCT.md — linkedn't

register: brand

## What it is

linkedn't is a Chrome extension that puts a **Deslop** button under every
LinkedIn post and rewrites the post into what the person actually meant.
Layoffs stop being journeys; "humbled and honored" becomes "look at me."
The landing page at linkednt.com sells the joke and the install.

## Users

People who read LinkedIn daily and are exhausted by it: job seekers,
engineers, recruiters who are in on the joke. They arrive from word of
mouth or a screenshot in a group chat. They get the bit within two
seconds or they leave.

## Brand voice

Deadpan, dry, specific. The site is a pitch-perfect parody of LinkedIn's
own UI: same feed cards, same blue, same typography energy. The comedy
lives in the copy and in small interactions (easter eggs, the marquee
confessions, the phrasebook). The design never winks first; the interface
plays it straight and lets the words land.

Three voice words: **deadpan, corporate, mischievous.**

## Aesthetic lane

"LinkedIn's uncanny valley." Not a tech-startup gradient page, not
editorial. It looks like the product it mocks: white cards on a
feed-gray background, LinkedIn blue (`--blue: #2d64bc`), system grotesk
body type, Plus Jakarta Sans only for the wordmark badge. Motion is
quiet and physical (existing easing: `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.7, 0.3, 1)`),
plus deliberate micro-delights: click sparks on Deslop, magnetic CTAs,
a middle-finger cursor.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages (gradient heroes, icon-card grids).
- Neon dev-portfolio effects, WebGL backgrounds, glassmorphism.
- Anything that breaks the LinkedIn-clone illusion.

## Strategic principles

- The demo IS the pitch: the hero card deck and the How section let the
  visitor click Deslop themselves before installing.
- Keep the parody airtight: real UI affordances, honest privacy story,
  no dark patterns.
- Every interaction should reward curiosity (clickable buzzwords,
  killable ghost phrases, escalating footer disclaimer).
