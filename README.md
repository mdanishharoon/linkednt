# linkedn't

LinkedIn, but it says what it means.

## The problem

Somewhere along the way, the world's largest professional network became the world's largest creative writing exercise. Layoffs became journeys. Jobs became callings. A man watched his daughter refuse broccoli and closed a B2B deal about it. Every quarter is incredible, every departure is a difficult decision made with immense gratitude, and every single person is humbled, which is a strange thing to announce about yourself in a public post with your face on it.

Nobody talks like this. Everybody posts like this.

## What linkedn't does

linkedn't puts a **Deslop** button under every post in your feed. Click it, and the post gets translated into what it actually says. The translation renders right where the post was, in a quiet little card, with a "Show original" button in case you miss the performance.

The poster is not notified. They keep posting into the void, blissfully unaware that anyone can now read them clearly.

## The voices

You pick how blunt you want it:

- **The Group Chat**: how the post reads when it's screenshotted into the group chat.
- **Touch Grass**: plain English for people who go outside.
- **TL;DR**: what happened, in one line. No lessons learned.

So "Reflecting on an incredible quarter of learnings, pivots, and exponential growth 📈" becomes, depending on your mood: a first-person confession about typing 📈 over a chart that goes sideways, a calm note that the quarter didn't go to plan, or simply: *the numbers were flat.*

## A small phrasebook

| They wrote | They meant |
| --- | --- |
| "I'm humbled and honored…" | Look at me. |
| "We're like a family here" | You will cry in the bathroom. |
| "Let's circle back on this" | I am begging you to forget this. |
| "Fast-paced environment" | You'll find out why the role is open. |
| "Thoughts? 👇" | I am so alone. |

There are roughly nine thousand more.

## What it doesn't do

It doesn't read your feed at large, only the posts you point it at. It doesn't tell anyone what you translated. It doesn't keep the text of anything. And it can't stop people from posting this way; it can only stop you from having to read it raw.
